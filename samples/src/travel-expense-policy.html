TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

Green Tea Demo Joint Stock Company (a fictional company used for illustration only)

Policy no. FIN-12. Effective 1 July 2026.

Section 1. Purpose and approval

1.1 Purpose. This policy explains which business travel costs the company pays for, how much it pays, and how employees claim the money back.

1.2 Scope. It applies to all employees and to contractors whose contract says that travel costs are reimbursed.

1.3 Pre-approval. Every business trip must be approved in the expense system before any booking is made. A trip that was not approved in advance may be refused for reimbursement.

1.4 Approval limits. A line manager can approve a trip budget up to USD 1,000. A department director approves a budget above USD 1,000 and up to USD 5,000. The Chief Financial Officer approves any budget above USD 5,000.

1.5 Booking channel. Flights and hotels should be booked through the company travel desk so that negotiated rates are used. An employee who books directly pays any difference above the company rate.

1.6 Personal travel. Personal days added to a business trip are at the employee's own cost. Any extra fare caused by the personal days is not reimbursed.

Section 2. Transport and accommodation

2.1 Flights. Employees fly economy class on flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed only on flights of 6 hours or longer and only with written approval from a Vice President.

2.2 Ground transport. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed when public transport is not practical. Car rental requires approval from the department director.

2.3 Private car. When an employee uses a private car for a business trip, the company pays USD 0.30 per kilometre, based on the distance shown in a mapping service.

2.4 Hotels and meals. The maximum amounts per night and per day depend on the tier of the destination city, as shown in the table below.

City tierHotel cap per nightMeal allowance per day
Tier 1 (capital cities and major financial centres)USD 140 per nightUSD 60 per day
Tier 2 (other large cities)USD 110 per nightUSD 45 per day
Tier 3 (all other locations)USD 80 per nightUSD 35 per day

2.5 Exceeding the caps. An amount above the cap is paid by the employee unless the department director approved the higher amount before the booking.

2.6 Stays. Employees should stay in hotels within reasonable distance of the work location. Serviced apartments are allowed for stays of more than 14 consecutive nights.

Section 3. Expense claims

3.1 Deadline. A claim must be submitted within 30 days after the end of the trip. Claims submitted later need a written reason and the approval of the Finance Manager.

3.2 Receipts. An original or scanned receipt is required for every expense above USD 25. Expenses of USD 25 or less may be claimed without a receipt but must be listed in the claim.

3.3 Payment time. The Finance team pays approved claims into the employee's payroll account within 10 business days.

3.4 Cash advance. An employee may request a cash advance of up to 50% of the approved trip budget. The advance must be settled in the first claim after the trip.

3.5 Foreign currency. Expenses in foreign currency are converted at the company exchange rate on the date of the expense. Fees charged by a bank for foreign card payments are reimbursed.

3.6 Items not reimbursed. The company does not reimburse alcohol, fines, hotel minibar charges, or personal entertainment.

Section 4. Compliance

4.1 Accuracy. Employees are responsible for the accuracy of every claim they submit. A false claim is treated as a serious breach of the code of conduct.

4.2 Review. The Finance team reviews a random sample of 10% of claims each quarter and may ask for original receipts.

4.3 Repayment. Any amount paid by mistake must be repaid within 15 business days of the notice from the Finance team, or it will be deducted from the next payroll.

4.4 Questions. Employees should send questions about this policy to the Finance team through the internal helpdesk.
